GoodNeighbour Child Safety Standards

App: GoodNeighbour (org.goodneighbour.app) · Developer: Aarohan Rural Development Society · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Our position

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). GoodNeighbour is for adults aged 18 and over. Any content that sexualises, abuses, exploits or endangers a child is forbidden and is removed.

What is not allowed

  • Images, videos, text or links that show or promote sexual abuse or exploitation of a child.
  • Attempts to contact, groom or exploit a child through the app.
  • Requests for help that use a child's image or story in a harmful or sexual way.

How we prevent it

  • Every post is reviewed by an administrator before other people can see it.
  • Accounts must be created with a verified email address or Google sign-in.
  • The app is not meant for anyone under 18 and is listed for adults only.

How to report a concern

  • In the app, open a post and tap Report. Choose a reason and send it.
  • Or email aarohandevelopmentsociety@gmail.com with the subject Child safety concern.

What we do when we receive a report

  • We review it quickly and remove the content.
  • We remove the account that shared it and keep the records needed for an investigation.
  • We report confirmed CSAE content to the authorities, including the local police, the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and the National Commission for the Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR), and we cooperate with their requests.

Legal compliance

We follow the laws of India that protect children, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.
